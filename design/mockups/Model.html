How Madad works

Four steps turn a monthly stock count into a transfer plan. Every number in Madad traces back to one of them.

1Forecast

Three XGBoost quantile models predict next month's use for every facility + product as a low (P10), likely (P50) and high (P90) value.

141 features: up to 12 months of history, district and national demand, season
2Detect

Compare stock to the high forecast. Shortfall = P90 − stock. Surplus = stock − P90.

Busy-month planning: a facility only counts as having surplus if it would still cover a busy month
3Redistribute

A linear program matches donors to receivers per product and month: first cover the most shortage, then keep distances short.

Distance from facility coordinates (haversine), 256 product-month problems
4Explain

An LLM turns results into plain language: answers, briefs and transfer requests. It reads results through tools and never creates numbers.

English and Arabic · sources shown on every answer

How to read a forecast

P10 19 P50 35 P90 78 stock 32
P10: only a 1 in 10 chance use is lower.
P50: the most likely value. Half the time it's more.
P90: only a 1 in 10 chance use is higher. Madad plans on this.
Stock line left of P90: the facility may run short in a busy month.
Example: Facility 780, Product 39, April 2023
